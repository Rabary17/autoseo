***To register in France a vehicle bought abroad, the tax clearance certificate remains a mandatory step. This free document, issued by the French tax authorities, confirms the vehicle is in order with French tax rules.***

Without it, you cannot get a permanent registration certificate. Here's who needs to request it, which documents to gather, and the pitfalls that most often hold up a file.

TL;DR: the tax clearance certificate is requested from the tax office, free of charge.

-   It is **free**, issued within 2 to 5 working days for a complete file.
-   A vehicle from a country **outside the EU** also needs a customs clearance certificate (846 A).
-   Vehicles already registered in France before export, light trailers and agricultural vehicles are **exempt**.

## What is the tax clearance certificate and who needs it?

**Direct answer**: the tax clearance certificate confirms your foreign vehicle meets French tax obligations, particularly on VAT. It is mandatory for any vehicle bought in the EU or outside it, before you can get a French registration certificate, and it stays valid indefinitely unless the vehicle is resold.

It covers new or used vehicles bought in the EU, vehicles imported from a third country such as the UK, Switzerland or Morocco, and classic vehicles unless specifically exempt. Conversely, trailers and semi-trailers under 500 kg, agricultural vehicles, diplomatic or consular vehicles, and vehicles already registered in France before their export are exempt.

For a vehicle from outside the EU, a **customs clearance certificate (form 846 A)**, issued by customs after payment of the applicable duties, must also accompany the tax clearance file.

## How to request a tax clearance certificate in 2026

Send your request to the **Business Tax Service (Service des Impôts des Entreprises – SIE)** for your area, online or in person, with a complete file.

1.  **Gather the documents**: Cerfa form n°13750\*08, a copy of the foreign registration certificate, purchase invoice or sales contract, proof of address less than 6 months old, proof of ID, and for a vehicle from outside the EU, the customs clearance certificate 846 A.
2.  **Submit the file** to the SIE, online via the tax website or in person.
3.  **Wait 2 to 5 working days** for a complete file; the certificate is then sent by email or post.
4.  **Present it** along with the rest of your file, including the [European Certificate of Conformity](/european-coc-vehicle-registration/), to finalise permanent registration. Our guide on [registering an imported vehicle](/register-imported-vehicle-france/) covers the rest of the process.

## EU vs non-EU imports: what actually changes

The tax clearance certificate is part of a wider file, whose content differs by the vehicle's origin. For a vehicle from the **European Union**, VAT has usually already been paid in the country of origin, or in France at purchase if the seller is a private individual and the vehicle is over 6 months old or has more than 6,000 km on it. The tax clearance certificate is still required to check your situation, with no customs duties or clearance involved.

For a vehicle from a **country outside the EU** (UK, Switzerland, Morocco), a prior customs file is needed, with import duties and VAT calculated on the vehicle's value, plus any CO₂-related taxes. The tax clearance certificate is only issued after this customs clearance, never before.

| Vehicle origin | VAT | Customs clearance required | Overall timeline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| European Union | Usually already paid | No | Comparable to a standard registration |
| Outside the EU | Due on import, calculated on value | Yes (certificate 846 A) | Noticeably longer |

*Source: Business Tax Service, 2026 procedures*

In both cases, plan for a longer overall timeline than a standard registration. For non-EU vehicles especially, the file cannot move forward until customs has completed its own part.

## What to do if your application is refused

A refusal most often comes from an incomplete file, unpaid taxes on the vehicle, or an error on the invoice or foreign registration certificate. The tax authorities always state the reason for refusal and the steps to fix your situation. If in doubt about a missing document, it's best to call the SIE before resubmitting an incomplete file a second time, which only extends the delay unnecessarily.

For a vehicle from outside the EU, check first that the customs clearance certificate 846 A is included in your file; its absence remains the most common reason for refusal on this type of import.

## Driving and insuring the vehicle while you wait

While your complete file (tax clearance certificate, certificate of conformity or individual vehicle approval, French MOT) is being processed, an imported vehicle can be driven on a temporary **WW registration plate**, issued by an authorised professional. Since the reform that took effect on 1 January 2026, this plate is valid for 4 months with no possible renewal, unlike the previous system which allowed an extension. Our article on the [temporary WW plate's validity](/temporary-ww-plate-validity/) covers this change and the steps involved.

This plate doesn't replace any of the permanent steps; it simply keeps the vehicle on the road while the file is processed. Its cost varies by the professional issuing it, an amount to add to the overall import budget alongside the tax clearance certificate (free), the regional tax, any ecological penalty and, for a non-EU vehicle, import duties and VAT.

Contrary to a common belief, you don't need to wait for the final registration certificate to insure an imported vehicle. Most insurers will open a policy based on the foreign registration certificate and the purchase invoice, updating the file once the French certificate arrives. Always declare the vehicle's foreign origin to your insurer from the start; an accident history abroad may be requested, and an incomplete declaration at this stage can complicate a future claim. Never drive, even on a temporary plate, without valid insurance: penalties for a lack of cover are separate from those for a missing registration certificate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the tax clearance certificate chargeable?

No, it's free, issued by the Business Tax Service within 2 to 5 working days for a complete file.

### Which vehicles are exempt from the tax clearance certificate?

Trailers and semi-trailers under 500 kg, agricultural vehicles, diplomatic or consular vehicles, and vehicles already registered in France before their export.

### Do you need an extra document for a vehicle imported from outside the EU?

Yes, the customs clearance certificate 846 A, issued by customs after payment of the applicable duties, which must accompany the tax clearance request.

### What should you do if your tax clearance application is refused?

First check the reason given by the tax authorities, most often an incomplete file or an unpaid tax, then complete the file before resubmitting it.

### Can you drive without the tax clearance certificate while waiting for the final registration?

Yes, thanks to a temporary WW registration plate, valid for 4 months with no renewal possible since 2026, issued by an authorised professional.

### Do you need the French registration certificate to insure your imported vehicle?

No, most insurers will open a policy based on the foreign registration certificate and the purchase invoice, to be updated once the French certificate is obtained.
