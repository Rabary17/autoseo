Thinking of renting a car from a private owner instead of a rental agency? In 2026, this option can save you 30 to 50% compared to traditional rentals. However, between insurance policies, mixed reviews and sometimes unclear terms, here are the key points to check before booking.

## Why choose peer-to-peer car rental? The real cost

For a Paris-Lyon trip (465 km), a compact saloon car costs an average of £80 per day with a traditional rental company. With a private owner via Getaround or Ouicar, expect to pay between £45 and £60. That’s a saving of 25 to 40%.

Another advantage: flexibility. Platforms offer slots ranging from one hour to several weeks. Perfect for a house move or a last-minute getaway.

## Included insurance: what Getaround (and others) really cover

Contrary to popular belief, [comprehensive insurance is mandatory and included](/en/shared-mobility/peer-car-rental-insurance-gaps) on platforms like Getaround. Partnering with AXA, it covers:

-   Damage to the vehicle, even if the renter is at fault.
-   Theft, subject to filing a police report.
-   Third-party liability, up to €100 million.
-   Repatriation costs in case of an accident.

Bear in mind: your personal car insurance **does not** cover this type of rental. So, checking with your insurer would be pointless. Only the platform’s coverage applies.

### Exclusions to be aware of

-   Damage to tyres and rims, unless caused by an accident where you’re at fault.
-   Theft of personal belongings left in the vehicle.
-   Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
-   Use on a racetrack or private circuit.

In case of an accident, an excess of €750 to €1,500 remains your responsibility, depending on the contract.

## User reviews: 3 recurring issues

On forums and review platforms, three criticisms come up regularly:

-   **Variable cleanliness** — Some owners don’t clean their vehicle between rentals. One user reported: \*« I picked up a car with crumbs in the seats and a stale cigarette smell. Fortunately, the platform refunded the cleaning fee. »\*
-   **Late returns** — 15% of delays are caused by previous renters returning the vehicle late. So, allow a 30-minute buffer.
-   **Meticulous vehicle checks** — A 2 cm scratch or a fuel tank not filled to 100% can result in extra charges. So, take photos before and after each rental.

### How to avoid unpleasant surprises

-   Read **recent** reviews (less than 3 months old). After all, an owner’s behaviour can change.
-   Check for unlimited mileage. Some contracts limit it to 200 km/day.
-   Opt for vehicles with a connected box (Getaround). That way, you benefit from keyless entry and GPS tracking.

## Peer-to-peer rental vs PCP/leasing: what’s the difference?

Unlike [peer-to-peer car-sharing](/en/shared-mobility/peer-to-peer-car-sharing), PCP (Personal Contract Purchase) and leasing are 2 to 5-year contracts. Here are the key differences:

-   **PCP** — You can become the owner of the vehicle at the end of the contract by paying a residual value. For example, €5,000 for a car rented at €300/month.
-   **Leasing** — Pure rental, with no option to buy. The vehicle is returned at the end of the contract.
-   **Peer-to-peer rental** — No minimum duration (from 1 hour to several weeks), no commitment.

For a one-off need, like a house move or a holiday, peer-to-peer car-sharing is far more flexible. However, for daily use, PCP or leasing may be more advantageous.

## 5 mistakes to avoid at all costs

1.  **Not checking the tyres** — Worn tyres (less than 1.6 mm tread depth) can result in a €135 fine. They may also immobilise the vehicle.
2.  **Forgetting to fill up** — Some owners charge up to €2 per litre if the tank isn’t full on return.
3.  **Ignoring zone restrictions** — Some cities, like Paris or Lyon, restrict access to vehicles with Crit’Air 3 stickers or higher. So, check the vehicle’s sticker before booking.
4.  **Not taking before/after photos** — In case of a dispute over a scratch or damage, photos are evidence. Take shots from multiple angles.
5.  **Renting without comparing insurance** — Not all platforms offer the same cover. Getaround includes 24/7 assistance, which isn’t always the case elsewhere.

## Our verdict: who is peer-to-peer car rental for?

✅ **Ideal for:**

-   One-off trips, like weekends or house moves.
-   Tight budgets. After all, savings can reach 30 to 50% compared to a professional rental.
\\n-   Drivers comfortable with digital tools. Booking and unlocking are done via an app.

❌ **Avoid if:**

-   You need a vehicle **immediately**. The booking process can take time.
-   You’re particular about cleanliness. There’s a risk of picking up a poorly cleaned vehicle.
-   You often drive in cities. Parking can be tricky.
