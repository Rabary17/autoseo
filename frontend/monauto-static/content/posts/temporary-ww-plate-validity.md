***Since 1 January 2026, the temporary WW plate looks different: a pink background, an expiry date printed on it, and above all a fixed duration of 4 months with no renewal possible for an imported vehicle.***

This reform replaces the old system, which allowed a 2-month extension. Here's what changed, what stayed the same, and how to avoid ending up with no valid registration halfway through a French registration file.

TL;DR: the temporary WW plate can no longer be renewed since 2026.

-   Standard duration for an imported vehicle: **4 months**, not renewable.
-   Duration for incomplete coachbuilt vehicles or self-propelled agricultural machinery: **6 months**, not renewable.
-   The plate is now **pink with black characters**, with the expiry month and year printed directly on it.

![Temporary WW plate fitted on an imported vehicle awaiting its definitive registration certificate](/images/articles/plaque-ww-provisoire-duree-1.webp)

## How long does a temporary WW plate last in 2026?

**A standard temporary WW plate, issued for an imported vehicle awaiting its definitive registration certificate, stays valid for 4 months, with no renewal possible**, under the rule in force since 1 January 2026. The old system allowed 2 months plus an automatic 2-month extension, for the same total, but that extension step has now disappeared.

This change doesn't shorten the maximum total duration for a standard import file, but it removes the extension step entirely. Once the 4 months pass, no new temporary certificate can be issued for the same vehicle: the definitive registration certificate must arrive before the deadline, or the vehicle can no longer be driven legally.

| Situation | Duration since 2026 | Renewable? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Imported vehicle awaiting definitive certificate | 4 months | No |
| Vehicle intended for export | 4 months | No |
| Incomplete coachbuilt vehicle or self-propelled agricultural machinery | 6 months | No |

*Source: order on temporary registration plates, in force since 1 January 2026.*

## Why did the WW plate turn pink, and what about old white plates?

The new WW plate shows a **pink background with black characters**, with the expiry date (month and year) printed directly on the plate, on the right. The change aims to make the plate easier for law enforcement to spot and to cut down on fraud: a vehicle still on the road past its printed deadline becomes immediately identifiable at a roadside check. **White plates issued before the reform stay valid until their own original expiry date**, with no requirement to replace them early.

## Which vehicles need a temporary WW plate?

A temporary WW plate stays mandatory for any vehicle without a definitive French registration certificate yet. That mainly covers:

-   Vehicles **bought abroad**, new or used.
-   Vehicles **imported from outside the EU**, awaiting their tax clearance certificate and certificate of conformity.
-   Vehicles that changed owner abroad and need a new French registration.

Our guide on [registering an imported vehicle in France](/register-imported-vehicle-france/) covers the full file, document by document, depending on where the vehicle comes from. For a vehicle from outside the EU, the [tax clearance certificate](/foreign-vehicle-tax-clearance-2026/) stays a required step before any definitive registration, alongside the European [certificate of conformity](/european-coc-vehicle-registration/).

A temporary WW plate cannot be used for a vehicle already registered in France. In that case, a new certificate goes through the standard registration procedure instead.

## How do you get a temporary WW plate in 2026?

The process runs entirely online, on ants.gouv.fr, in a few steps.

1.  **Gather your documents**: proof of identity, proof of address under 6 months old, European Certificate of Conformity, purchase invoice or transfer certificate, and a temporary insurance certificate.
2.  **Log into your ANTS account**, or create one if needed.
3.  **Select "Request for a temporary registration certificate (CPI)"** under the registration section, and attach the required documents.
4.  **Validate and pay the application fee**, around €13.76 in 2026.
5.  **Receive your certificate** electronically, usually within 5 to 10 working days. Order the physical plate separately afterward from an approved professional.

Once you receive the temporary certificate, you have **1 month** to fit the physical WW plate to the vehicle. Past that point, the certificate becomes invalid.

## What insurance do you need, and what if the definitive certificate is late?

Driving on a temporary WW plate without valid insurance stays strictly prohibited, and can mean a fine of up to **€3,750** plus the vehicle being immobilised. Insurance must cover at least **third-party liability**, for the whole duration of the plate, and most insurers will open a policy based on the foreign registration certificate and purchase invoice, without waiting for the final French certificate.

Since the WW plate can no longer be renewed, a slow-moving file turns urgent within the first few weeks. Three things matter most: **actively chase your file** with the ANTS or the professional handling it, checking progress in your personal account; **check for any pending document**, especially the tax clearance certificate for a non-EU vehicle, often the piece that holds a file up the longest; and **never drive past the deadline printed on the plate**, since doing so risks immediate immobilisation at a roadside check.

## Mistakes to avoid, and what it costs

Five mistakes cause most of the trouble. **Waiting until the last week to chase the file**: with no renewal possible, a delay of a few days can leave you with no valid registration at all. **Skipping temporary insurance**: its absence costs up to €3,750 in fines, on top of the vehicle being immobilised. **Confusing the WW plate with a definitive registration**: it doesn't remove any step of the process, it only keeps the vehicle on the road while the file is processed. **Forgetting the European Certificate of Conformity**: without it, the application gets rejected outright. **Driving without the printed or digital certificate**: it must be shown at every roadside check, just like a standard registration certificate.

| Expense item | Estimated cost |
| --- | --- |
| ANTS application fee | €13.76 |
| Physical WW plate | €20-50 |
| Temporary insurance (4 months) | €50-150 |
| Certificate of Conformity | €100-300 |

*Source: ants.gouv.fr*

The total budget for a temporary WW plate, excluding the Certificate of Conformity, generally lands between **€80 and €250**, depending on the options chosen.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a temporary WW plate last in 2026?

For an imported vehicle awaiting its definitive registration certificate, the duration is 4 months, with no renewal possible since the reform took effect on 1 January 2026.

### Can a WW plate still be extended beyond 4 months?

No. The automatic extension that used to exist has been removed. Past the deadline, only the definitive registration certificate allows the vehicle to keep being driven legally.

### Why is the WW plate now pink?

To make it easier for law enforcement to spot and to cut down on fraud. The expiry date, month and year, appears directly on the plate.

### Is a temporary WW plate valid for a vehicle already registered in France?

No. It's reserved for vehicles with no definitive French registration yet, mainly those imported from abroad.

### Do you need specific insurance during the WW plate's validity period?

Yes, at least third-party liability insurance, valid for the plate's whole duration. Its absence risks a €3,750 fine and the vehicle being immobilised.

### Which vehicles qualify for a WW plate valid for 6 months?

Incomplete new vehicles intended for coachbuilding, and self-propelled agricultural machinery, whose duration was also doubled but remains, just the same, non-renewable.
