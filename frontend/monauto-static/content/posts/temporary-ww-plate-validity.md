A temporary WW registration plate allows you to drive an imported vehicle in France while waiting for your definitive registration certificate. According to service-public.gouv.fr, its validity period is strictly regulated. Here’s everything you need to know in 2026 about its duration, renewal, and the steps to follow.

![White temporary WW registration plate fitted on an imported vehicle awaiting its definitive registration certificate](/images/articles/plaque-ww-provisoire-duree-1.webp)

## Maximum validity of a temporary WW registration plate in 2026

In France, a temporary WW registration plate is valid for \*\*4 months maximum\*\* from its issue date, as specified by service-public.gouv.fr. This period can be extended \*\*once only\*\* for \*\*an additional 2 months\*\*, making a total of \*\*6 months\*\*. After this period, the vehicle can no longer be driven legally in France without definitive registration. This rule applies to all imported vehicles, whether they come from the European Union or a non-EU country. The registration process for an imported vehicle is detailed in [our guide on vehicle registration and imports](/en/vehicle-registration-legal/import-registration).

## When and how to renew a temporary WW registration plate

Renewal is not automatic. It must be requested \*\*before the current plate expires\*\*, ideally \*\*15 days before the end of its validity\*\*. The application is made online via the ANTS website or through an approved professional. Thus, the first extension (from 4 to 6 months) is possible only once. The application must be complete and submitted on time. Processing time is \*\*5 to 10 working days\*\* to obtain the new temporary certificate, as stated on ants.gouv.fr.

## Which vehicles require a temporary WW registration plate?

A temporary WW registration plate is mandatory for all imported vehicles without a definitive French registration certificate. This includes:

-   Vehicles purchased abroad (new or used).
-   Vehicles imported from a non-EU country.
-   Vehicles that have changed ownership abroad and require new registration in France.

Note: a temporary WW registration plate cannot be used for a vehicle already registered in France. In this case, a new registration certificate must be requested via the standard procedure.

## Mandatory insurance for a temporary WW registration plate

Driving with a temporary WW registration plate without valid insurance is strictly prohibited. According to legifrance.gouv.fr, this can result in severe penalties, such as a fine of up to €3,750 or the immobilisation of the vehicle.

-   **Mandatory temporary insurance:** the vehicle must be insured at least for \*\*third-party liability\*\*.
-   **Coverage period:** the insurance must cover the entire validity period of the WW plate, including any extension.
-   **Insurance certificate:** the certificate must be presented in the event of a roadside check.

## Steps to obtain a temporary WW registration plate in 2026

Obtaining a temporary WW registration plate is mainly done online via the ANTS website. Here are the key steps.

### 1\. Prepare the necessary documents

-   Proof of identity.
-   Proof of address less than 6 months old.
-   European Certificate of Conformity.
-   Purchase invoice or vehicle transfer certificate.
-   Proof of import (if the vehicle comes from a non-EU country).
-   Temporary insurance certificate.

### 2\. Create an account on ants.gouv.fr

Go to the official website and create an account if you haven’t already done so.

### 3\. Submit the WW plate application

Log in to your personal account. Then select the “Registration” section followed by “Request for a temporary registration certificate (CPI)”. Fill in the online form and attach the required documents. Finally, validate and pay the application fee (\*\*€13.76 in 2026\*\*).

### 4\. Receive the temporary certificate

The temporary certificate is usually issued within \*\*5 to 10 working days\*\* after the application is validated. It is sent by email and must be printed or kept in digital format. The physical WW plate must then be ordered separately from an approved professional. Once the temporary certificate is received, you have \*\*1 month\*\* to have the WW plate fitted to your vehicle. After this period, the certificate becomes invalid.

## What to do if the definitive registration certificate is not obtained within 6 months

If the definitive registration certificate is still not issued after the 6-month validity period of the WW plate, the vehicle can no longer be driven legally in France. Several solutions are available.

-   **Request an exceptional extension**
    
    In some cases, an extension may be granted by the prefectural services. This request must be supported by official documents.
    
-   **Register the vehicle abroad**
    
    If the French registration certificate is not obtained within the time limit, you can apply for temporary registration in the country of purchase. The vehicle can then be re-imported into France with a new WW plate.
    
-   **Sell the vehicle as is**
    
    If no solution allows the registration certificate to be obtained in time, selling the vehicle to a professional may be an option.
    

In all cases, driving without a valid plate or definitive registration certificate is prohibited. Penalties can include the immobilisation of the vehicle.

## Temporary WW registration plate: mistakes to avoid at all costs

-   **Forgetting to take out temporary insurance**
    
    Driving without valid insurance can result in a fine of €3,750 and the immobilisation of the vehicle.
    
-   **Not respecting the renewal application deadlines**
    
    A renewal application must be submitted before the current plate expires. A late application will be rejected.
    
-   **Using a WW plate for a vehicle already registered in France**
    
    A temporary WW registration plate is reserved for imported vehicles awaiting their French registration certificate.
    
-   **Neglecting the vehicle’s compliance**
    
    The European Certificate of Conformity is mandatory to obtain a WW plate.
    
-   **Driving without the printed or digital temporary certificate**
    
    The temporary certificate must be presented in the event of a roadside check.

## What to do in case of loss or theft of the temporary WW registration plate

If your temporary WW registration plate is lost or stolen, follow these steps.

-   Report the loss or theft to the police and obtain a receipt.
-   Submit a new application for a temporary certificate on ants.gouv.fr. Attach the receipt and a new insurance certificate.
-   Order a new physical WW plate from an approved professional.

## Temporary WW registration plate vs. definitive WW plate: key differences

Do not confuse the temporary WW registration plate with the definitive WW plate, which is valid for 1 year. Here are the main differences.
